### New

- While the computer is not connected, a turn still in progress shows as last known: no running timer or working indicator.
- Bot DMs show in the app as they happen instead of every 10 to 30 seconds.

- A message you sent no longer stays pinned at the bottom of the chat under later replies after you come back to a busy chat.
- Photos sent over a slow connection no longer time out and start again from the beginning.
- A message that keeps failing to send says why ("Retrying: …") instead of looking like it is still on its way.
