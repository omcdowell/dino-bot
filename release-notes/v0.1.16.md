### New
- Attach videos from your library (up to 200 MB) or take a photo with the camera from the chat's attach menu. The bot gets each video's path, type and duration.
- Files you send show in the chat as chips with an icon, name and size, and stay on your message; tap one to download it again.
- Chat images show whole, never cropped. Long-press an image to save or share it.
- The desktop app says when a new version is out, and `dinobot update` tells you on a Mac when the desktop app is older than the new release.

### Fixed
- Sending many large photos no longer freezes the app: they upload one by one before the message, and a retry sends only what is left.
- A computer that is already signed in (its own login or a proxy) is no longer asked to sign in again after you pair again.
- A Claude Code bot no longer merges a stopped message into your next one, and its text before a tool call stays whole.
- One Claude usage card per computer instead of two.
